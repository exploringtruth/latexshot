Lives in your menu bar. Customize the shortcut, choose your clipboard format, and enable Launch at Login in Settings. Printed math comes first; handwriting support is planned.

Screen Recording permission is needed to read your selection. Download updates manually from GitHub Releases. The frozen printed-equation benchmark accepted 60 of 66 results (90.9%). Review the copied LaTeX before using it. Benchmark results

Privacy & Offline Guarantee

Offline Recognition: Equation crops stay in memory during processing and are recognized entirely on-device by the bundled offline model and engine. Images and equations are never uploaded or transmitted to any server.

Screen Recording Permission: Screen recording access is used exclusively to capture the exact screen rectangle you select during capture.

Local Preferences and Equation History — Custom clipboard wrappers, shortcut configurations, and preferences are stored locally on your device in macOS preferences. Equation History stores recent copied formula text on your Mac across restarts.

Network Connections: Send Feedback… opens GitHub Issues in your browser. Version 1.0.0 uses manual downloads and has no automatic update checks or analytics.
